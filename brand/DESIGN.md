# AI Gym identity

Practice makes possibility.

## Direction

Elastic open G with lifted terminal: practice and measured progress. Generous breathing room, a strong grid and sparing energetic accents

Open G becomes Spring tail curl and lifted paw; companions vary natural silhouettes. The mascot is an original character, not a literal logo export. Spring leads a completed five-identity avatar pack. Companion silhouettes vary while preserving the contract's material and color grammar.

## Color and type

Semantic roles are authoritative in tokens.json and tokens.css. Light backgrounds use #F4F8F6; dark backgrounds use #0B2020. Light actions use #006B58; dark actions use #76E4C0. Do not flatten the dark palette into the light palette. Preserve system high-contrast behavior in the native application.

Manrope is supplied under SIL OFL 1.1 in logo-kit/fonts. Native application controls use Windows system fonts. The logo wordmarks are outlined SVGs; do not recreate their spacing with live text.

## Voice

Encouraging, precise and curious; never competitive or anthropomorphically overconfident

Short bio: A place to practice with local AI. Shape a dataset, train an adapter, compare held-out results, and keep the evidence close.

Use concrete verbs, visible state and truthful results. Never describe a saved configuration as a running model or a passing test as a model-quality guarantee.

## Assets

The logo kit and the five avatars are in this folder. The eight reaction animations and their light stills ship with the app in src/AiGym/Assets/Brand/reactions.

## Usage

Use the primary, secondary, symbol, monochrome and reversed roles from logo-kit; its BRAND-GUIDE.md defines clear space, minimum sizes and misuse rules. Preserve the outlined wordmark. graphics/USAGE.md documents social crops, maskable safe areas and export dimensions.
