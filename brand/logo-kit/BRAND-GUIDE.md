# AI Gym brand guide

## Brand foundation

**Positioning:** An encouraging, rigorous workshop for model practice and experimentation

**Attributes:** distinctive, clear, capable, welcoming

## Logo system

- Use `brand-primary-outlined.svg` for the default identity.
- Use `brand-secondary-outlined.svg` when the descriptor is unnecessary.
- Use `brand-symbol.svg` for square placements and social avatars.
- Use `brand-mono.svg` when only one production color is available.
- Use `brand-reversed.svg` on dark brand-color backgrounds.
- The wordmark is custom outlined artwork. Do not recreate or typeset it.

Minimum digital width: **180 px** for the primary lockup and **24 px** for the symbol.

Clear space: Keep at least one symbol stroke width clear on every side.

## Typography

Logo lettering: Manrope 750, outlined with HarfBuzz. Do not retype the logo.

Supporting typography:

- **Manrope** (As supplied, SIL Open Font License 1.1).

Manrope for identity; Windows system font for application controls.

## Do

- Preserve the supplied proportions, spacing, colors, and artwork.
- Use outlined SVG for production and transparent PNGs for raster-only systems.
- Preserve the documented clear space and minimum size.
- Use the approved reversed mark on dark fields.

## Do not

- Stretch, rotate, redraw, or alter approved component geometry.
- Recolor components without an approved revision.
- Add shadows, outlines, or effects to the supplied mark.
- Typeset a replacement for an outlined wordmark.

## Governance

Model-assisted artwork is not trademark clearance; qualified legal review remains a separate step.
